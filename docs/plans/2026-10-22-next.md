# decide after 2.0: agreed scope

Source: a panel of 6 personas (designer, tech lead, indie dev, backend dev on SSH, freelancer, founder; 3 Claude Sonnet + 3 Codex), then the owner's swipe round `1-983ce7` on 2026-10-22: 40 cards, 4 skipped.

One release. decide stays on 127.0.0.1 and in the browser.

## In

1. **Round export.** Save a round as one self-contained HTML file: questions, options, media and answers inside, opens without decide and without network.
2. **Answer in the file.** The recipient answers inside that HTML and downloads an answers file; the agent imports it into the round.
3. **Several answer files** (low priority, may be cut): the agent merges files from different people and shows where they disagree.
4. **ADR export.** A finished decision becomes `docs/decisions/*.md` (context, options, decision, consequences). The journal itself stays in `~/.decide`.
5. **Journal page** with search and filters.
6. **Superseded decisions.** A decision can be marked superseded; `history` warns the agent when it is about to contradict a live one.
7. **Attention budget.** "I have 5 minutes": the agent asks only what matters most and lists what it left out.
8. **"Only my answer" flag** on a question: the recommendation is never filled in for it.
9. **System notification** when a round opens or the agent replies on the page.
10. **Swipe hesitation mark.** A long pause or a taken-back answer reaches the agent as "not sure or hesitated", no other logic.
11. **HTML block** in a question, sandboxed like `preview`, height fitted to content. Raw HTML in text stays escaped (5 of 6 personas against it).
12. **Mockups:** width switch 375 / 768 / 1440; before/after slider for two live mockups; marker notes on top of a mockup.
13. **Simulator capture:** `screenshot` from the iOS Simulator and Android emulator, not only URLs.
14. **Two P3s from the 2.0 review:** a `|` inside a link breaks a table cell (`ui/js/md.js`); focus stuck in a mockup iframe stops the arrow keys (`ui/js/swipe.js`).

## Out

Hosting links, phone over Wi-Fi, `ssh -L` hint, terminal client, stable decision IDs in commits, journal in the repo, "accept all recommendations", auto-decide on timeout, a shared inbox tab, manual sure/guess marks, editing answers after Done, "my pick before the recommendation", mockup state tabs, Figma frames, price/date totals, revisit conditions (later).

## Undecided (skipped)

Project rules "don't ask about X" from an answer; "measured" evidence on options; rehearsal of options in worktrees; "what shipped" check.
